Edit your cake menu

Change a name or price below, or add a new cake. Prices are in rupees. Enter prices for ½ kg, 1 kg or one piece; leave unavailable sizes blank. Cakes with the same category appear together.

To apply changes: click Download updated menu, then replace menu.js in your website folder with the downloaded file. Reload your website to see the changes. For a hosted website, upload the replacement menu.js too.

This editor works locally. It does not save changes to your website automatically. Keep this editor on your computer; you do not need to upload it.
